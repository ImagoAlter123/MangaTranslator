MangaTranslator

New installation: extract the ZIP, run INSTALL_ALL.cmd, then RUN.cmd.
Update: close the app and replace the program files. Preserve .venv, models,
runtime and your projects. Existing models do not need downloading again.

DOWNLOAD_LAMA.cmd installs LaMa only.
REPAIR_INSTALLATION.cmd repairs pip in the app environment.
INSTALL.cmd installs the basic editor; INSTALL_AI.cmd runs the full installer.

PDF/PNG export allows text outside the selection box. Brush size is remembered.
See README.md for details.
